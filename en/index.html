What we do

Three focused areas.

01

Analyses and valuations

We produce financial analyses, business plans and company valuations for owners, boards, buyers and investors. Our work is grounded in robust assumptions, the realities of the Icelandic market, and a measured view of operations, cash flow and enterprise value.

Common engagements

  • Valuations and financial analyses for owners
  • Valuations for acquisitions or mergers
  • Business plans for financing
02

Financing

We support companies and owners through financing — whether equity, debt or a combination. Our work spans preparation, investor presentations, and dialogue with banks, funds and individual investors.

Common engagements

  • Companies in active fundraising
  • Owners in dialogue with banks or investors
  • Refinancing or debt restructuring
03

Brokerage

Figaro helps domestic and international parties build connections in the Icelandic financial market. The service includes market analysis, presentation preparation, meeting coordination and follow-up with professional and institutional investors.

Common engagements

  • International parties interested in the Icelandic market
  • Asset managers introducing themselves to Icelandic institutional investors
