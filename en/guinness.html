Guinness Global Investors

Figaro represents Guinness Global Investors in Iceland — an independent asset management firm headquartered in London.

The partnership focuses on the introduction and distribution of foreign equity funds to Icelandic institutional investors. Guinness manages equity funds with a clear investment philosophy and an emphasis on quality companies that deliver consistent returns and offer long-term growth potential. Portfolio managers Ian Mortimer and Matthew Page have long managed Guinness's flagship funds in this area.

01

Global Equity Income

Y EUR Acc · ISIN IE00BVYPNZ31 · OCF 0.77%

An equity fund invested in 35 selected quality companies, with each holding equally weighted in the portfolio. The emphasis is on consistent dividends and long-term dividend growth. The strategy targets companies that have delivered consistent, high returns over extended periods.

Key characteristics

  • 35 companies equally weighted, low turnover
  • Focus on long-term dividend growth over high yield
  • Mortimer and Page managers since launch in 2010

Global Innovators

Y EUR Acc · ISIN IE00BQXX3L90 · OCF 0.79%

A growth fund invested in 30 selected quality companies, with each holding equally weighted in the portfolio. The emphasis is on alignment with long-term growth themes. The strategy targets companies with high return on capital, strong balance sheets and disciplined valuation.

Key characteristics

  • High return on capital and strong balance sheets
  • Mortimer and Page managers since launch in 2014
